CHANGES to Pen Ball, medium ball, medium pen hole (makersmakingchange/Pen-Ball), file Medium_Pen_ball_Medium_Pen_hole.stl (two halves, our 01a and 01b)
Made 2026-10-17 by the 3D Print Portfolio (Adaptive Workshop) build session.
Why: we print and sell this part. The owner asked that Makers Making Change marks come off the part itself.
Credit to the designer and Makers Making Change goes in the listing and the insert card instead (see assistive/ATTRIBUTION.md).

What was removed:
  On both halves: the text "MAKERS MAKING CHANGE", a small cross mark and the letters "M/M" (the MMC logo letters), cut about 0.6 to 0.8 mm into the lower band of the dome, all the way round from about 84 to 324 degrees, 0.9 to 5.2 mm above the bed. (An earlier note called this raised text. Measured, it is cut in, not raised.)

How:
  Filled. The band is a 30 sided turned shape (flat facets, not a smooth round). It was rebuilt all the way round as the exact 30 sided stack read from the corner points of a clean facet, between 0.5 and 6.0 mm up, and swapped in for the old band (method loft in assistive/builds/mmc_strip/mmc_fill.py). On the facets that never had text, the new band matches the old one to 0.0000 mm, so the rebuilt facets carry the same flats and edges as the rest of the ball: no flat spot and no step.

Files:
  source/, prep/ and numbered/ now hold the cleaned files. Our debossed number is unchanged.
  with-mmc-marks/ holds the files exactly as they were before this change.
