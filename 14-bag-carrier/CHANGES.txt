CHANGES to Bag Carrier (makersmakingchange/Bag-Carrier), file Bag_Carrier_Medium.stl
Made 2026-10-17 by the 3D Print Portfolio (Adaptive Workshop) build session.
Why: we print and sell this part. The owner asked that Makers Making Change marks come off the part itself.
Credit to the designer and Makers Making Change goes in the listing and the insert card instead (see assistive/ATTRIBUTION.md).

What was removed:
  The letters "MMC", cut about 0.8 mm into the outside of the back bar (+Y side, x -9.5 to 9.5 mm, z 4 to 10.6 mm).

How:
  Filled. The bar is a rounded profile swept along a gentle curve, so the outer surface in a box around the letters was rebuilt as that same profile (taken from the clean columns at x = -12 and +12 mm) plus the bar's curve (taken from the clean rows above and below the letters), and the box was replaced with it (method heightfield in mmc_fill.py). Largest step at the patch edge 0.016 mm, highest point 0.006 mm above the old one.

Files:
  source/, prep/ and numbered/ now hold the cleaned files. Our debossed number is unchanged.
  with-mmc-marks/ holds the files exactly as they were before this change.
  This is a modified version of a share-alike design. The modified files stay under the same license as the original.
