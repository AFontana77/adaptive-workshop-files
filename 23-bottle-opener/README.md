What we changed (2026-11-05): we removed the words "MAKERS MAKING CHANGE" from the outer edge of the handle on all 5 sizes, and filled that part of the wall flush. The rest of each part is the same, and the size numbers stay. The method was: rebuild the straight outer wall from a clean slice just below the letters, over the band the letters sat in, and leave the rounded inner edge alone.

Files: Bottle_Opener_30_changed.stl, _35, _40, _45 and _50.
